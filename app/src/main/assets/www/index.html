Road to Riches

Your ride is one of your biggest monthly costs. Buy a car, lease one, or go car-free — make the real decisions, dodge the traps, and see what each choice costs over 5 years.

Tips for teachers

  • Starter (ages 14–18) shows hints and walks through the math. Real World hides hints and scores the math questions.
  • Play the same character in a big city and a small town, then compare the 5-year costs.
  • Tap any underlined term for a plain-English definition.
  • A full game with all three paths takes about 15–20 minutes.

Free, trustworthy help

  • Consumer Financial Protection Bureau auto loan guides: consumerfinance.gov.
  • Federal Trade Commission car buying and leasing tips: consumer.ftc.gov.
  • Safety recalls for any car, by VIN: nhtsa.gov/recalls.
  • Your local credit union can preapprove a car loan before you visit a dealer.

About

Road to Riches™: Lease It or Own It or Hoof It is a free transportation and wealth game from WGRALGO / The Wealth Gap Resolution Algorithm™ Inc. Buy a car, lease one, or go car-free, then see what each choice costs over 5 years.

For education only — not financial or legal advice. All numbers are examples; real prices, rates, insurance, fares, and laws vary by place and change over time.
